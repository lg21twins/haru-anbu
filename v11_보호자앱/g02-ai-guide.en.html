General health information. Assessment and diagnosis of a resident's condition are made by the care team's medical staff.

Q I'm worried about side effects from my parent's medication
A

Headache, dizziness, and indigestion are common early reactions. If severe allergic reactions, difficulty breathing, or loss of consciousness occur, seek medical care immediately. Inform the pharmacist and attending doctor of all current medications to check for interactions.

Q How do I read blood pressure and blood sugar readings?
A

Normal blood pressure is below 120/80 mmHg; 130 or above is pre-hypertension. Fasting blood sugar below 100 mg/dL is normal; 126 mg/dL or above meets the diagnostic threshold for diabetes. An actual diagnosis requires a physician. Home readings are most accurate taken at rest after waking in the morning.

Q What diet is recommended for a hypertension patient?
A

Keep sodium under 2,000 mg per day (about 5 g of salt). Limit soup broth, avoid processed foods, and eat potassium-rich foods like bananas, potatoes, and tomatoes regularly. Blue-backed fish is recommended at least twice a week.

Q What exercises help prevent falls?
A

Try chair-assisted single-leg stands, heel raises, and sit-to-stands. Just 10–15 minutes a day can improve lower-body strength and balance, significantly reducing fall risk.

Q How do I apply for a long-term care grade?
A

Apply by visiting a National Health Insurance Service branch, calling 1577-1000, or through the NHIS website. Assessment typically takes about 30 days from submission.

Q Are there government programs that help cover care costs?
A

Qualifying for a long-term care insurance grade unlocks home care and facility care benefits. Additional programs include family care allowances, special care benefits, and inpatient nursing support.

Q What can I do when I'm completely exhausted as a family member?
A

Family burnout is a common experience. Try to carve out even 30 minutes of personal time each day, and make use of respite care services such as short-term residential stays and day programs.
